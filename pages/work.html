Work

Case studies that show how I think, decide, and execute.

This section is set up to become the strongest part of the site. For now, it includes a reusable structure you can fill in with real projects.

Case Study Template

  1. Context: What was the business or product situation?
  2. Problem: What needed to be solved?
  3. Role: What did you own or influence?
  4. Actions: What did you do and why?
  5. Outcome: What changed?
  6. Learnings: What would you carry forward?

Suggested case study themes

  • A product launch or major feature release
  • A prioritization or roadmap decision
  • A user problem you clarified and solved
  • A cross-functional execution challenge
  • An AI-related exploration with product relevance

What strong case studies should demonstrate

  • Clear thinking under ambiguity
  • Good product judgment and tradeoff awareness
  • Ownership and cross-functional collaboration
  • Impact on users, teams, or business outcomes
  • Ability to learn and improve over time

Next step

Professional story

I work at the intersection of product thinking, execution, and communication. I’m interested in understanding the real user problem, shaping a sensible path forward, and helping teams move with focus.

My aim with this site is to show not just what I’ve worked on, but how I approach decisions, priorities, and outcomes.

How I think about product

  • Start with the problem, not the feature.
  • Good prioritization comes from clear tradeoffs.
  • Strong product work needs both strategy and execution.
  • Communication is part of product quality.
  • AI is useful when it solves real workflow problems.

Areas I want this site to reflect
